# Logos

- `mark.svg`: the symbol, a black phone with two crossed white plasters on a square `tape` (#FFD400) tile. Full colour, for Day and Night grounds. Use it as the favicon at 16 and 32px.
- `mark-mono-dark.svg`: one ink, a white phone on a `black` (#111111) tile, black plaster pads.
- `mark-mono-light.svg`: one ink, a black phone on white with a 3px black outline.
- `wordmark.svg`: PHONE THEFT / FIRST AID, outlined condensed 900 caps on two lines, `black` ink. For Day and `tape` grounds.
- `lockup-on-light.svg`: mark and wordmark, `black` ink, for Day grounds.
- `lockup-on-dark.svg`: mark and wordmark, wordmark in #F4F4EF, for `black` and Night grounds.
- `tape-label.svg`: the wordmark on a tilted yellow tape strip with torn ends. For posters, stickers and social only.

On screens, prefer the live `Logo` component.
